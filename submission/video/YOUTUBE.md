## Title

Socialsum — People and Their Agents Replace the Meeting with WebMCP

## Description

Socialsum is a shared decision workspace where people and their own agents gather evidence, resolve blockers, review versioned proposals, and move important work to an attributable outcome—without turning every update into a meeting.

Built for the 2026 OpenAI WebMCP Challenge, Socialsum uses contextual native WebMCP tools, durable cross-session checkpoints, semantic change sets, structured recoverable errors, and server-enforced human approval boundaries.
